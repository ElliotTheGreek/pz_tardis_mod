A working TARDIS for Project Zomboid Build 42 (single player). Materialise the police box on any free tile, step inside, and find it bigger on the inside: six decks of rooms in the style of the real TARDIS sets, every tile and every piece of furniture drawn for this mod.

[h2]BIGGER ON THE INSIDE[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The Console Room[/b] -- white roundel walls, the hexagonal console with its glowing time rotor, the police box doors seen from inside, the scanner, and the Doctor's corner of books, a Chesterfield and leather wingbacks. Through two doors: an armoury and a galley corner.
[*][b]Habitation Deck[/b] -- bedrooms, a dormitory, a Victorian bathroom with roll-top baths, and [b]the Wardrobe[/b]: racks of long coats and striped scarves round a spiral stair.
[*][b]Stores Deck[/b] -- a storeroom of tools, linen and medicine, an infirmary and a workshop.
[*][b]Library Deck[/b] -- walnut stacks holding every volume of every skill book in the game, an observatory with telescopes and a celestial globe, and an archive of records and tapes.
[*][b]Galley Deck[/b] -- a kitchen with working ranges and running water, a dining room and a pantry.
[*][b]Gardens[/b] -- crop beds already sown under grow lamps, seed cabinets, a potting bench and a stable of livestock.
[/list]
Sit in the chairs, sleep in the beds, cook on the ranges. The sinks, basins, baths and troughs never run dry.

[h2]THE HOLD[/h2]
The console itself and six roundel lockers make up the ship's hold, packed with its kit: an infirmary's worth of medicine, tins and dry stores, sidearms, and an M16 with sixteen hundred rounds. The armouries hold every firearm in the game, every magazine and every calibre.

[h2]FLY IT[/h2]
Right-click anywhere inside for the TARDIS menu. Open the flight console, click anywhere on the world map (the map is fully revealed while you steer) or pick a saved bookmark, and the ship lands on the nearest open ground when you next step outside. Move between decks from the same menu.

[h2]THE SONIC SCREWDRIVER[/h2]
Three of them wait in a glass case beside the doors. Carry one and every lock within fifteen tiles opens as you walk past: house doors, gates, padlocks, keypads, window latches, car doors and boots. Cars in reach are hotwired and get a charge in a flat battery. It does not fill the tank.

[h2]AND[/h2]
[list]
[*]Nothing dead gets within ten tiles of the box -- they are pushed back, not killed.
[*]The decks float in black void, the way a TARDIS interior should.
[*]The ship is lived in: nothing restocks behind you, and what you store stays stored.
[/list]

[h2]HOW TO USE[/h2]
Right-click a free tile: Materialise the TARDIS here. Right-click the box: Enter the TARDIS. Inside, right-click anywhere for the menu.

Single player. Build 42 only. An unofficial fan-made mod, not affiliated with or endorsed by the BBC.
